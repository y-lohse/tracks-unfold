# Imitation

## Role

Develop hearing and reproduction of pitch relationships through generated phrase-imitation puzzles. Use the shared lives-and-runs structure. This note describes learning intent and the high-level concept, not the detailed prototype implementation.

## Player action

Hear a short reference phrase, then reconstruct its pitch relationships on the chromatic spiral using a supplied response anchor. Depending on the demands, reproduce movement direction or more precise interval sizes. The anchor can place the response in another register or at another pitch.

A response succeeds when it satisfies the stated pitch-relationship constraints. Different demands can be exercised together and their progress tracked separately. Concrete generation settings, listening allowances and rating formulas are not specified here.

## Learning coverage

- [[P1 - Pitch direction]]: distinguish and reproduce higher, lower and repeated pitches through phrase movement.
- [[P2 - Octaves and note identity]]: encounter octave distances and relationships between registers; octave-shifted responses offer an opportunity to connect differently registered phrases.
- More precise reconstruction exercises auditory interval reproduction. Naming intervals is not a requirement of this type and need not be added to it; structural interval naming belongs in [[Note navigation]].

This type focuses on listening and reproduction, not note-name questions or simultaneous-interval identification. It does not need to cover the entire interval strand.

## Octave weakness

Reproducing an octave leap or an octave-shifted phrase does not by itself establish recognition of the same note identity across octaves. The current concept therefore supports P2 only partially.

Design octave-related examples and feedback that make the equivalence perceptible rather than assuming players infer it. The precise treatment remains open. Do not declare auditory octave equivalence demonstrated solely because the player completes shifted reconstruction.

## Open design work

Clarify how octave-related variations teach and demonstrate P2. Retain the core hearing-and-reproduction interaction rather than adding terminology tasks to fill unrelated coverage gaps. The existing run-director prototype is a reference, not a requirement to reproduce every parameter or implementation detail.

| Foundation               | What the player starts learning                                                                | Existing skills        |
| ------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Pitch relationships      | Hear higher, lower and repeated pitches; recognize octave relationships.                       | P1–P2                  |
| Navigating notes         | Understand the repeating note sequence, octaves, semitones, whole tones and accidentals.       | P3–P5                  |
| Pulse and duration       | Distinguish pulse from rhythm; understand notes and silence as durations relative to the beat. | R1–R3                  |
| Subdivision and grouping | Divide beats and locate events within recurring beat groups.                                   | R4–R5                  |
| Intervals                | Hear, construct and name distances between notes, connecting sound with structure.             | I1–I3                  |
| Tonal orientation        | Hear a home note and relate notes to it using a supplied major scale and degree labels.        | Introductory K1 and S2 |

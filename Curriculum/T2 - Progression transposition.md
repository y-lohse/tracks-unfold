# T2 - Progression transposition

## What this skill enables

Realize the same Roman-numeral progression in different keys, preserving chord qualities and harmonic relationships.

## Builds on

- [[H2 - Roman-numeral progressions]]
- [[H3 - Minor-key chords and progressions]]

These are direct learning foundations, not requirements for perfect mastery. Backlinks show skills that build on this one.

## Scope and design notes

Introduce major progressions before the complete minor coverage is available. Transposition offers recurring practice in musical relationships, not only an end-of-curriculum application.

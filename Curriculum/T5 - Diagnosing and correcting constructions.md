# T5 - Diagnosing and correcting constructions

## What this skill enables

Diagnose and correct a musical construction that violates an explicit requirement, identifying the relationship that needs changing.

## Builds on

Depends on the specific construction being used; see scope below.

These are direct learning foundations, not requirements for perfect mastery. Backlinks show skills that build on this one.

## Scope and design notes

Conditional prerequisite: the construction skills actually used in the task. A correction is right because it meets explicit requirements, not because unconventional music is inherently wrong.

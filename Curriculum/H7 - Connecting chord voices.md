# H7 - Connecting chord voices

## What this skill enables

Connect successive chords using common tones and small movements; choose inversions or voicings to meet an explicit movement constraint.

## Builds on

- [[C5 - Voicing and doubling]]
- [[H2 - Roman-numeral progressions]]

These are direct learning foundations, not requirements for perfect mastery. Backlinks show skills that build on this one.

## Scope and design notes

Use explicit constraints such as retaining common tones or limiting movement. Do not replace those constraints with an opaque artistic-quality score.

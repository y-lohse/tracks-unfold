# I3 - Hearing intervals

## What this skill enables

Distinguish common interval sounds in successive and simultaneous notes; connect the heard relationship to its construction and name.

## Builds on

- [[I2 - Interval names and construction]]

These are direct learning foundations, not requirements for perfect mastery. Backlinks show skills that build on this one.

## Scope and design notes

Develop listening alongside scales and chords. Immediate recognition of every isolated interval is not a gate before learning chord construction.

# C1 - Major and minor triads

## What this skill enables

Construct and label major and minor triads as root, third and fifth; identify their roots and qualities from their notes. Recognize common minor-symbol variants such as Cm, Cmin and C−. Compare the audible effect of changing a major third to a minor third while holding the other notes constant.

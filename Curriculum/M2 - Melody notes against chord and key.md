# M2 - Melody notes against chord and key

## What this skill enables

Identify whether a melody note belongs to the current chord; separately determine whether it belongs to the key.

## Builds on

- [[C1 - Major and minor triads]]
- [[H1 - Diatonic triads in major]]

These are direct learning foundations, not requirements for perfect mastery. Backlinks show skills that build on this one.

## Scope and design notes

Chord membership and key membership are separate questions. A note outside the current chord is not automatically outside the key or musically wrong.

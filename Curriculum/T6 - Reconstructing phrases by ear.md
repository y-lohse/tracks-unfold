# T6 - Reconstructing phrases by ear

## What this skill enables

Reconstruct the pitches and rhythm of a short heard phrase using a supplied tonic or starting pitch.

## Builds on

- [[I3 - Hearing intervals]]
- [[S2 - Scale degrees]]
- [[K1 - Hearing the tonic]]
- [[M1 - Melodic movement and contour]]
- [[R4 - Beat subdivisions]]
- [[R5 - Meter and downbeats]]

These are direct learning foundations, not requirements for perfect mastery. Backlinks show downstream skills.

## Scope and design notes

Allow repeated listening and a pitch reference; do not test absolute pitch or require staff notation. Start with short, simple major-key phrases; add minor and richer rhythm as those supporting skills develop. Connect heard notes to the tonic as well as to adjacent notes.

## Examples of use

Reconstruct an unfamiliar short phrase with the correct relative pitches, event timing and rests. Pitch and rhythm can be practiced separately as well as together, allowing attention to each before combining them.

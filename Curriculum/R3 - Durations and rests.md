# R3 - Durations and rests

## What this skill enables

Construct and recognize durations and rests as fractions or multiples of a beat; account for the full duration of a pattern, including silence.

## Builds on

- [[R1 - Pulse and rhythm]]

These are direct learning foundations, not requirements for perfect mastery. Backlinks show skills that build on this one.

## Scope and design notes

Introduce duration names and rests without requiring staff-pitch reading. Success must account for silence, not only attacks.

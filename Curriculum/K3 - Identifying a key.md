# K3 - Identifying a key

## What this skill enables

Identify the key of straightforward passages using tonic, scale content and cadential evidence together—not one isolated clue.

## Builds on

- [[K2 - Major and minor tonal contexts]]
- [[H4 - Tonal endings and cadences]]

These are direct learning foundations, not requirements for perfect mastery. Backlinks show skills that build on this one.

## Scope and design notes

Use passages with a clearly established key. Introductory tonic listening can start much earlier; reliable key identification also needs harmonic evidence. Ambiguous excerpts and modulation are outside this initial scope.

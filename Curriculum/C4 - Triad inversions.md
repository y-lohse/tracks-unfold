# C4 - Triad inversions

## What this skill enables

Recognize and construct root position, first inversion and second inversion; distinguish the root from the bass note.

## Builds on

- [[C1 - Major and minor triads]]

These are direct learning foundations, not requirements for perfect mastery. Backlinks show skills that build on this one.

## Scope and design notes

Inversion depends on the lowest sounding note, not the ordering of every upper note. The bass note and root are different concepts.

# R5 - Meter and downbeats

## What this skill enables

Track recurring beat groups and the downbeat; construct patterns that fit a stated meter, initially 2/4, 3/4 and 4/4.

## Builds on

- [[R3 - Durations and rests]]
- [[R4 - Beat subdivisions]]

These are direct learning foundations, not requirements for perfect mastery. Backlinks show skills that build on this one.

## Scope and design notes

Initially focus on 2/4, 3/4 and 4/4. Time-signature vocabulary does not require reading pitches on a staff.

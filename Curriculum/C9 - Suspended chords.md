# C9 - Suspended chords

## What this skill enables

Construct sus2 and sus4 chords; explain which note replaces the third and distinguish suspension from an added note. Compare the audible effect of replacing a triad’s third with the second or fourth.

## Builds on

- [[C1 - Major and minor triads]]
- [[S2 - Scale degrees]]

These are direct learning foundations, not requirements for perfect mastery. Backlinks show skills that build on this one.

## Scope and design notes

The scope is sus2 and sus4 as chord-symbol constructions. Historical suspension treatment and mandatory resolution are not required here.

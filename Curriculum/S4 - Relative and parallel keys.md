# S4 - Relative and parallel keys

## What this skill enables

Distinguish relative major/minor from parallel major/minor: shared note collection versus shared tonic.

## Builds on

- [[S3 - Natural minor]]

These are direct learning foundations, not requirements for perfect mastery. Backlinks show skills that build on this one.

## Scope and design notes

Relative major and minor share a note collection but have different tonics. Parallel major and minor share a tonic but differ in note collection.

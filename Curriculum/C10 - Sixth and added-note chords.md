# C10 - Sixth and added-note chords

## What this skill enables

Construct common sixth and added-note chords, including 6, m6, add9 and m(add9); distinguish their additions from triad tones. Compare the audible effect of the added note against the original triad in controlled examples.

## Builds on

- [[C1 - Major and minor triads]]
- [[I4 - Compound intervals]]

These are direct learning foundations, not requirements for perfect mastery. Backlinks show skills that build on this one.

## Scope and design notes

Distinguish adding a note from replacing the third. Start with complete, explicit constructions rather than professional omission conventions.

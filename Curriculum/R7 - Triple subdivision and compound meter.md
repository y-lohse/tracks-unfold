# R7 - Triple subdivision and compound meter

## What this skill enables

Distinguish division into two from division into three; interpret simple triplets and a basic 6/8 pulse grouped into two main beats.

## Builds on

- [[R4 - Beat subdivisions]]
- [[R5 - Meter and downbeats]]

These are direct learning foundations, not requirements for perfect mastery. Backlinks show skills that build on this one.

## Scope and design notes

A later foundational target. Distinguish three subdivisions within a main beat from three main beats in a bar. Defer advanced polyrhythms and extensive odd-meter study.

# T3 - Chord construction on unfamiliar roots

## What this skill enables

Construct an unfamiliar chord from a familiar symbol type on a new root, rather than retrieve a memorized note shape.

## Builds on

Depends on the specific construction being used; see scope below.

These are direct learning foundations, not requirements for perfect mastery. Backlinks show skills that build on this one.

## Scope and design notes

Conditional prerequisite: the construction skill for the chord type being used, from C1–C11. These are alternatives selected by the task, not a requirement to complete all chord skills first. Vary roots without introducing untaught chord types.

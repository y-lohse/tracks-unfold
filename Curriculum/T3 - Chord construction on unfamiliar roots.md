# T3 - Chord construction on unfamiliar roots

## Ability

Construct an unfamiliar chord from a familiar symbol type on a new root, rather than retrieve a memorized note shape.

## Builds on

Depends on the specific construction being assessed; see scope below.

These are direct learning foundations, not requirements for perfect mastery. Backlinks show skills that build on this one.

## Scope and teaching notes

Conditional prerequisite: the construction skill for the chord type being assessed, from C1–C11. These are alternatives selected by the task, not a requirement to complete all chord skills first. Vary roots without introducing untaught chord types.

## Evidence of understanding

Assess the ability above in more than one example. Distinguish supported performance from independent performance, and naming from hearing or construction where relevant. A multi-skill task only provides evidence for relationships the player actually has to determine.
